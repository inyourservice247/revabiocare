# Real Content Checklist

## Required for launch
- Company: legal description, operating model, approved overview, public contact details.
- Products: name, category, CAS, grade, available documentation, approved description, public-display approval.
- Services: YES/NO, approved name, scope, geography, approved description.
- Quality/compliance: evidence-backed licences, certifications, document types and claims.
- Legal: privacy wording, legal entity details and required disclosures.

## Optional if management wants it displayed
- Founding year, management story, values, markets served, roles and brand assets.
